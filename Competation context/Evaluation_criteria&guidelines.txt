📢 PROMPTWARS 2026 | Evaluation Criteria & Guidelines

Dear Participants,

As previously communicated, there are no restrictions on the AI tools you choose to use during PromptWars 2026.

To ensure a consistent and transparent evaluation process, all submissions will be assessed using the same six parameters:

1.⁠ ⁠Code Quality – Structure, readability, and maintainability.
2.⁠ ⁠Security – Secure and reliable implementation.
3.⁠ ⁠Efficiency – Performance and resource optimization.
4.⁠ ⁠Testing – Functionality and reliability.
5.⁠ ⁠Accessibility – Usability and inclusiveness.
6.⁠ ⁠Problem Statement Alignment – How effectively your solution addresses the given challenge.

💡 Additional Consideration: Google Technologies

Participants are encouraged to explore Google technologies such as Firebase, Google Cloud, Gemini, and Google AI Studio.

The effective and relevant integration of these technologies may positively contribute to the overall quality of your solution under the applicable evaluation criteria. However, simply using a particular service does not guarantee additional marks.

The evaluation focuses on what you build, not which tool you use.

A powerful AI tool alone does not guarantee a strong solution. What matters is how effectively you use the available resources to develop a functional, well-implemented application.

PromptWars is about smart and effective prompting, creative problem-solving, and effective execution.

We encourage everyone to participate confidently, make the most of their preferred tools, and focus on building the best possible solution.

All the best!!!

Team PromptWars 2026
